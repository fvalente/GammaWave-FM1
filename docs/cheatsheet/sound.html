MACROS M1–M4

Hold LFO: the knobs are M1–M4, latched until LFO again. Each session starts with 2 random routes a track; F3 rolls new ones, G3 clears. By hand: MOD, SRC M1…M4. Saved with the project.

SEQUENCER

64 steps a track, up to 4 notes a step, piano roll and drum grid. Hold a step + turn a knob: a parameter lock. Step recording: hold a chord + KNOB 1 its length; + ARP: arpeggiated (K1 rate, K2 order, K3 oct). COMP / COMP 2: two components a step (CHANCE, RATCH, ARP NOTES / SEQ / SCALE, TRIG, RAND, OCT, TONAL, BEND, ECHO, PULSE, JUMP, REV: TIMES x1 x2 1:2, FILL, KILL); a TRIG or FILL wins; RATCH / ECHO: DIV; WHEN: each one's loops (ALWAYS 1:2 2:2 SKIP) and the step's LEN. SHIFT's CHANCE thins the whole track; C.LEN gives the components a length of their own (they drift over the steps). REC + knobs while playing: automation (64 records a section, MOTION page plays / clears them, AUTO LIST edits them).

SCALE · ARP

ROOT and SCALE are every melodic track's: CHR MAJ MIN DOR MIX PEN MPEN HARM PHRY LYD LOC MEL BLUES WHOLE DIMHW DIMWH. QNT snaps keys or the sequence, TRN transposes, ALL TR (SCL 2) moves every sequence along the scale. CHORD: a key plays a chord (TRIAD 7TH ADD9 SUS2 SUS4 in key, MAJ MIN 7 MAJ7 MIN7 POWER), VOICING LEAD leads each to the next, STRUM as a harp.

SONGS

8 projects × 4 sections (A–D), a song of up to 16 rows by bars (SAVE › ARRANGE; END: STOP, x2–x8, LOOP), SLOOP style. Each row can hold a scene: mutes, DRUM-X group mutes, macros and MIDI punch-ins. SAVE held plays sections live on the next bar, stores them, records the song (SONG REC). Each section keeps its sounds, steps, motion, kit and its SLICER.

MASTER

CLIPPNCHDUCKREL

CLIP saturates without getting louder. PNCH drives the drums: denser body. DUCK: the kick pulls the rest down (REL its release). An always-on leveler (slow 2:1, −9…+6 dB) before the limiter keeps patches at a steady volume.
