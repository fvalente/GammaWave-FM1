THE PANEL

KNOBS AND TRANSPORT

MASTERMaster volume.
SELECTScrolls the section's pages (HOME: the 49-genre generator). Held with a button, its dice (OK keeps, CANCEL back): HOME the generator, EDIT the sound, SEQ the sequence, LFO the LFO, FX the effects. GLO + SELECT: the tempo. In the generator: its pages, the reel, the SET (round).
PRESETSBrowses the sounds (factory and user). On step, list and song pages: the cursor / the row. In the generator: the TAKES reel (scroll, round: the take plays on the next bar; OCT+ held: the keys make takes). Hold a page button + PRESETS: jump through its pages.
ALGORITHMSelects the track from any page: T1–T4, or T1–T6 with TRACKS 6. In the generator: new instruments for the piece (the notes kept).
KNOB 1–4The four columns on screen. Fast sweeps the whole range, slow fine-tunes. With a layer held: the layer's.
OCT− · OCT+The keyboard's octave (both together: back to 0). On action pages and dialogs: back / confirm.
PLAYStart / stop. GLO + PLAY: restart from the top.
RECArms live recording on the selected track (notes and knob moves, overdub).
HOMETap: the ecosystem. Hold: the MENU.
KEYS27 keys play the selected track in its scale; with a layer held they become that layer's.

GEN (OCT− + PLAY), any page; PRESETS: the TAKES reel. Hold OCT+: the keys are silent, each makes a child take from the one playing, on the next bar (stopped: at once); the screen draws this keyboard, the keys breathe. White F3–D4, let go: that part’s notes made again (with KNOB 4 on a section: there only); the black key beside it: its sound (the notes kept). Hold a part or sound key ½ s: lock it (ALGORITHM, ALL SOUNDS and JAM leave it; a new seed keeps it). DROP keys mute that part while held (no take). On another take’s card: the key borrows its part. PRESETS with OCT+ held: a cue, played when OCT+ is let go. D#5 JAM: a take by itself; held ½ s: every 4, 8, 16, 32 bars. F#5: the take’s share code.

GLO + G4UNDO (on the GEN screen too).

Each card: its ID (#0A3D), the take it came from and what changed (<0A3C LEAD SND), seed · BPM · key, two bars of every track, the song’s sections and the playhead. Every change says what it did in a pop-up mid-screen. The reel (32 takes), its SET and JAM are kept over power-off.

LFO · MACROS

Knobs M1–M4; they stay latched when let go (LFO again: off). F3 rolls new routes, G3 clears them (only tracks that sound). LFO + SELECT: the track's LFO diced.

GAMMAWAVE [OCT−] SHIFT SHORTCUTS & BUTTON COMBOS

OCT− + ARPHEARTH MACROS (1/6–6/6): Gravity, Singularity, Photosphere, Wraith Scream, Formant Vowel, Solar Eclipse — drive type and amount, cutoff, resonance (4–6: SCREAM / VOWEL filter). Undo: OCT− + GLO.
OCT− + PLAY49-GENRE SONG GENERATOR (GEN): Trap ATL, UK Drill, Dark Trap, Rage Trap, house, techno, IDM, ambient… KNOB 1 genre, KNOB 2 seed, KNOB 3 energy, KNOB 4 form; SELECT pages (parts, SONG: tempo key scale length, FEEL: space fills wet grit), ALGORITHM new instruments, PRESETS the TAKES reel (every piece made, kept over power-off: scroll to play one, K1 tempo, K2 key, K3 energy, K4 a section, SAVE keeps, EDIT deletes; hold OCT+: F3..D4 a part made again, black keys its sound, G4..F5 drop a part while held, F4 harmony, D#5 JAM, F#5 the share code; REC a SET); OCT+ makes, OCT− back, PLAY auditions.
OCT− + ENV / SCLSEQ > MUTATE (1-CLICK): jumps to MUTATE; if it was off, switches it on (Sound 2 bars, Pattern 4 bars, Amount 50%).
OCT− + HOME / SAVEHYPERSPACE VISUALIZER: 4 animated scenes (Supernova, Wraith 3D, Warp Tunnel, Matrix FFT); OCT+ next scene, OCT− leaves.
OCT− + SEQ · EDIT · FX · LFO1-HAND DICE: rolls Sequence, Sound Patch, Track FX, or LFO on the active track.
OCT− + GLOUNDO, again: REDO (same as GLO + G4).
